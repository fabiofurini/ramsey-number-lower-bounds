Explicit evidence.

Each construction avoids a blue triangle and a red clique of the prescribed size. A verified graph on N vertices proves R(3,n) ≥ N+1.

These are the results reported in the accompanying paper. They are presented with their certificates and do not constitute a live list of world records.
